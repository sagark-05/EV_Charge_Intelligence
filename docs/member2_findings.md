# Member 2 — temporal demand and recorded-energy findings

**Sagar K, PES2UG24CS424.** Results use 200,000 accepted sessions from 1 January 2024 through 30 December 2025: 730 inclusive dates with observed starts. Calendar averages assume complete collection, including zero-session dates; timestamps do not verify uptime or complete first/last-day coverage. Source status: **shared by team member; original source and licence unverified**. Methodology and timezone are unverified.

Hourly demand is **nearly uniform**. The project-defined relative index ranges from 0.97668 to 1.01760. Hour 08 has the most starts (8,480; 11.62/calendar day), and hour 09 the fewest (8,139; 11.15/calendar day), around a mean of 11.42 per hour per day. These rankings do not prove congestion. Daily starts range from 230 on 11 October 2025 to 344 on 26 January 2024. The trailing seven-day mean begins on the seventh date; the initial six values are deliberately missing.

Weekdays average **274.58 starts/day** across 522 dates, versus **272.45** on weekends across 208 dates: a descriptive difference of 2.13 starts/day (about 0.78% relative to weekends). Wednesday is highest at 276.83 per represented Wednesday and Sunday lowest at 271.14. The weekday–hour extremes are Wednesday 02 (12.27) and Tuesday 09 (10.52). Different groups use their own calendar denominators; raw weekday/weekend totals are not directly comparable.

Monthly average starts/day range from **270.00 in April 2024** to **281.13 in December 2025**. December covers only 1–30 December (30/31 dates), so it is explicitly marked as a partial boundary month rather than missing measurements. Among full calendar months, April 2025 is highest at 278.83/day. These descriptive differences do not establish seasonality, sustained growth, causes or statistical significance.

All 200,000 recorded-energy values pass finite, nonnegative numeric validity. Their total is **9,493,219.61 kWh**, averaging 47.47 kWh per valid-energy session. Monthly energy/day ranges from 12,780.06 kWh in September 2025 to 13,319.24 kWh in partial December 2025. Full session energy is assigned to session-start hour/month, including cross-boundary sessions: the profiles are **not measured hourly electricity load**.

Physical plausibility remains separate from numeric validity. Energy exceeds reported battery capacity in **49,101** sessions and nominal charger energy in **71,740**, with **23,488** in both groups. The exclusion sensitivity scenario removes their union of **97,353**, retaining **102,647 sessions (51.32%)** and **3,648,439.11 kWh (38.43% of baseline energy)**. Retained energy has observations on all 730 represented dates; that does not imply complete collection. Exclusions can change sample composition, and this scenario is not corrected or ground-truth data. Baseline demand is unchanged; unverified field definitions, losses and equipment information prevent automatic physical corrections.

The methodological motivation is Baek, K., Lee, E., & Kim, J. (2024), [*A dataset for multi-faceted analysis of electric vehicle charging transactions*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-02942-9), Scientific Data, 11, 262. Temporal inspection is adapted as an analysis idea; the paper is neither the source of our CSV nor proof of our numerical results. Session starts differ from connection/coupling profiles, and our normalization/index are project design choices.
